Hue & Seek — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · App ID: com.hueseek.game

Hue & Seek: Prop Hunt Online is designed to be fun, lightweight, and privacy-respecting. We do not sell your personal information and we do not require an email address, phone number, or social login to play.

1. Information We Store

  • Gameplay Progress (Local & Optional Cloud Save): Your chosen in-game gamertag, level, XP, tokens, unlocked cosmetics, and preferences are stored locally on your device. When you are online, an anonymous cloud backup (linked only to a randomly generated 20-character recovery code) is stored in Google Firebase Firestore so you can restore your progress on a new device.
  • Online Multiplayer Matchmaking: During live 5v5 online matches, your temporary match state (display name, character selection, position, and gameplay events) is relayed in real time via Photon Realtime Cloud or secure WebSocket relays. Match positions are ephemeral and are not stored after a match ends.
  • Anonymous Gameplay Analytics: To understand which worlds and modes players enjoy, how many matches are played, and where players drop off or hit bugs, the game sends anonymous gameplay event summaries (linked only to a random device install ID, never your gamertag, email, or advertising ID) to Google Firebase Firestore. Deleting your data in-game clears your local analytics ID.
  • No Sensitive Personal Data: We do not collect your real name, email address, precise GPS location, contacts, photos, or microphone/camera recordings.

2. Offline Mode

If your device does not have an internet connection, Hue & Seek automatically runs in Offline Mode with smart AI teammates and opponents. All progress stays safely saved on your device.

3. Deleting Your Data

You can permanently delete both your Cloud Save backup and all local save data at any time directly inside the game: tap the ☁️ Cloud Save button in the Lobby and tap 🗑️ Delete Save Data (tap twice to confirm). You may also clear app data or uninstall the app on your device.

4. Children's Privacy

Hue & Seek does not collect personally identifiable information from players of any age and contains no free-text global chat.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries or support requests, visit oxadow.com/hueseek.
